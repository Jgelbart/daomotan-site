Melodies

    Brief for musicians

    Goes on the session sheet, with the chords and the four tracks.

    Send to musicians

    • .htmlThe session sheet: tempo, key, chords bar by bar, the four tracks, every note and your brief. It prints.
    • .musicxmlThe score, chords on top. Opens as sheet music in MuseScore, Sibelius, Finale, Dorico.
    • .midFour tracks on four channels. Opens in any DAW to change sounds or rearrange.
    • .wavThe whole song, and each track alone. Drops into Unity, Godot, Unreal, GameMaker.

    Backup

    Melodies autosave in this browser only. Save a backup file to keep them safe or move them to another computer.

    Saved on NAN II

    Melodies save to NAN II itself as you write, and go into NAN II's backup.

    To download sound files, open Melody Cartridge from NAN II in a browser on the Mac.
