Meet NAN II, the robot nanny who keeps your family's world at home.

What NAN II does

Plays, plans and translates for the whole house.

Your music

NAN II plays the songs you own, from your own collection. No streaming account and no ads between tracks.

Your movies

Pick a film from the family library and NAN II plays it on its own screen, with sound from its own speakers.

Movie night on the wall

Dim the lights and NAN II projects the movie onto a wall or a hung-up bedsheet. Popcorn is your department.

Arcade games

Built-in arcade games with a proper controller, for kids after homework and grown-ups after bedtime.

The family datebook

Birthdays, pickups, piano lessons. NAN II keeps the family datebook and can tell you what's coming up.

Translator

NAN II listens in one language and speaks in another, so grandparents, sitters and new friends can talk.

And a little more: a friendly face on its own screen, a voice you choose, and a few instruments to play along with.

Kept at home

A good nanny keeps the family's business private.

NAN II does its thinking on its own computer, not on someone else's. That's what we mean by personal: NAN II belongs to your family, not to a cloud.

Works without the internet

NAN II listens, thinks and talks on board. When the Wi-Fi goes down, movie night doesn't.

Your memories stay home

The datebook, the music, the movies and what NAN II hears all stay in your house. Nothing is sent off to be stored or sold.

You're in charge

Every setting is yours to see and change, and nobody else can switch NAN II off from far away.
