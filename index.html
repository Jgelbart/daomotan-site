Meet NAN II, the robot that never phones home.

NAN II plays your music and movies, reads you books, looks things up in an offline Wikipedia and translates, all on board, with the AI models you choose. It works without the internet, and DAOMSYS, its operating system, never calls home to us. Next come its wheels, for the groceries and the campsite.

What NAN II does

Plays, knows and goes.

Plays

Your music

Plays the songs you own, from your own collection. No streaming account, no ads.

Your movies

Plays films from your own library on its screen, with sound from its own speakers.

Movie night on the wall

Dim the lights and NAN II projects the movie onto a wall, or a bedsheet strung between trees.

Arcade games

Arcade games designed by NAN II's inventor, played with a proper controller. Winner picks the next movie.

Knows

Wikipedia on board

An offline copy of Wikipedia lives inside NAN II. Look things up anywhere, with no signal at all.

A medical wiki

A medical encyclopedia on board, for looking things up when you're far from a signal. It's a reference, not your doctor.

Hundreds of books

Hundreds of classics from Project Gutenberg that NAN II can read to you, a chapter at a time.

Translator

Listens in one language and speaks in another, so everyone at the table can talk.

Make it yours

Pick its brain, its voice and its face.

Choose its brain

NAN II is the body. You choose the brain.

NAN II runs open AI models, the kind anyone can download, right on its own computer, and you decide which ones. DAOMSYS is the harness around them: it gives the model you pick ears, a voice, a face and a screen, and it decides what that model can reach. What the model says is up to the model and the people who made it, so choose one you trust.

  • Load the models you like, and change them whenever you want.
  • Use a quick model for easy chat and a deeper thinker for harder questions.
  • When a better open model comes out, put it on NAN II yourself. No waiting on us.

Pick its voice

Over two hundred voices to choose from. Listen to each one before you decide.

Design its face

A face editor is built in. Draw the expressions NAN II makes on its screen.

An owner's manual inside

Every button, game and setting is explained right on NAN II's own screen. No website needed.

No phone home

DAOMSYS never phones home.

“No phone home” means NAN II’s operating system never calls us: no telemetry, no account to sign into, no switch we can flip from afar. It doesn’t need the internet to work, and it answers to you.

It doesn't call us

DAOMSYS sends us no recordings, no usage data and no check-ins. There's no telemetry to opt out of.

No internet needed

Wikipedia, the books, the music and the movies are all on board, so NAN II works the same at home or off the grid.

Its memory stays home

NAN II keeps facts only when you tell it to, plus a day log of what it did and what its camera saw. Both stay on the robot, and you can ask what's in them.

Nothing changes unless you say so

DAOMSYS takes no updates from afar and leans on no servers of ours. Nothing changes, and it keeps working, until you decide otherwise.

What you add is your call

Install a different model, an app or a service that goes online, and it runs by your rules. DAOMSYS won't stop you, and it won't tell us.

Yours to repair or modify

Open it up, swap a part, change its voice, its face or its software. It's your robot.

Where NAN II comes from

NAN II was a story before it was a robot.

Every robot starts somewhere. NAN II started on the page.

  1. January 2014

    Written and drawn

    Jody Morris Gelbart wrote and illustrated a short story about a robot named NAN II, and registered it with the U.S. Copyright Office on January 29, 2014.

  2. The years between

    The world caught up

    The story waited while computers got small enough to ride inside a robot, and AI got good enough to run with no cloud at all.

  3. Off the page

    Designed by the person who dreamed it up

    Jody brought NAN II to life, and kept going: Jody designed every one of its arcade games and wrote its theme music, so NAN II's games and theme song are its very own.

  4. Today

    In the workshop

    NAN II talks, plays, reads and translates, and its wheels are being built. It's in the workshop now, being tested.
