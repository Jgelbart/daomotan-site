Meet NAN II, the robot that never phones home.

NAN II plays your music and movies, reads you books, looks things up in Wikipedia, translates, carries the groceries and comes camping. It does all of it on board, with no internet connection and no one listening in.

What NAN II does

Plays, knows and goes.

Plays

Your music

Plays the songs you own, from your own collection. No streaming account, no ads.

Your movies

Plays films from your own library on its screen, with sound from its own speakers.

Movie night on the wall

Dim the lights and NAN II projects the movie onto a wall, or a bedsheet strung between trees.

Arcade games

Built-in arcade games with a proper controller. Winner picks the next movie.

Knows

Wikipedia on board

Wikipedia lives inside NAN II. Ask it anything, anywhere, with no signal at all.

A medical wiki

A medical encyclopedia on board, for looking things up when you're far from a signal. It's a reference, not your doctor.

Hundreds of books

Hundreds of classics from Project Gutenberg that NAN II can read to you, a chapter at a time.

Translator

Listens in one language and speaks in another, so everyone at the table can talk.

Goes

Drive it

Pick up the controller and drive NAN II around the house, the yard or the campsite.

Carries your groceries

Load it up at the car and NAN II rolls the bags into the kitchen.

Take it camping

Music, movies, books and Wikipedia work just as well in the woods. No signal needed.

Comlink

A handheld comlink lets you talk to NAN II from across the house or across the campsite.

And a little more: a friendly face on its own screen, a voice you choose, and a few instruments to play along with.

No phone home

NAN II never phones home.

No reporting back, no internet connection, no account to sign into. Everything NAN II knows lives inside NAN II, and it answers to you.

Nothing gets reported back

NAN II doesn't send recordings, usage data or check-ins anywhere. There's nothing to opt out of.

No internet needed

Wikipedia, the books, the music and the movies are all on board, so NAN II works the same at home or at the campsite.

It remembers what you tell it to

Birthdays in the datebook, the shopping list, where the tent pegs went. NAN II keeps what you ask it to keep, and nothing else.

Yours to repair or modify

Open it up, swap a part, change its voice, its face or its software. It's your robot.
